# Owned teams in the compound mission world

[Issue246](https://github.com/lictl/WebRA2/issues/246) joins the existing
[fixed-actor team runtime](mission-team-runtime.md) to actual
[mission ownership actions](mission-house-dispatch.md). The opt-in policy is
`webra2-fixed-actor-team-action-world-1`. Complete original campaigns remain gated.

A source mission with both teams and house operations now requires a genuine owned
team binding for the complete source world. This retains combat, infantry slots,
navigation, health and every original actor. Each compound tick advances the common
team/world runtime exactly once, derives cell crossings and health callbacks from
that private successful step, and then runs the VM against a private action context.
This ordering remains an explicit D03 scheduling policy.

Ownership actions run at their actual VM boundary through the common team runtime.
They are never applied again from an effects list. A later population or
owner-dependent callback predicate sees the changed world immediately. An action4
request generated by that poll becomes eligible on the next tick. Claims and
releases retain their ownership revision; an active-member ownership change aborts
the entire candidate transaction. A no-op transfer retains its explicit history.

The private action context requires the complete matching team checkpoint when the
program contains team actions. It rejects copied runtimes, mismatched worlds,
pending transactions, wrong clocks and omitted team state. Success and failure both
consume the context. The compound publishes matching outer and nested world saves,
team histories and VM state only after every phase succeeds. Restoring or replaying
reconstructs those joins. New bounded restoration/admission wrappers account for
owned history work without changing the old unowned API results or identities.

Fixed actors can use the existing source cell/object catalogs, since neither their
identity nor membership grows. Crossings come only from completed movement; births,
reservations and unchanged positions are not crossings. Positive health loss drives
the existing callback sequence. Lethal health and completed death remain separate:
the original combat policy retains occupancy and population until retirement.
An actor's tag, type and damage-family identity remain source-bound while each
owner-sensitive predicate reads current ownership.

Spatial targets resolve against the same private candidate at their action boundary.
The compound returns ordered spatial requests and preserves the existing versioned
intent store. Indefinite positional loops survive save/restore; finite starts remain
receipts and are not replayed merely by restoring a checkpoint. The additional
current-world restoration for intent pruning is bounded and charged. No browser
audio consumer or native mixer timing is added here.

The complete source gate still rejects unsupported constructors, active captures,
tagged recruitment and unproved post-transfer mission eligibility. In particular,
the supplied Sleep recruitment rule is not overridden. Constructors and the
stationary Guard witness are separate work under
[245](https://github.com/lictl/WebRA2/issues/245) and
[247](https://github.com/lictl/WebRA2/issues/247). A component binding cannot skip
unknown mission instructions, enable an original campaign, or grant a native tag
cleanup/production/selection implementation.

Seven original miniature tests cover both profiles, same-poll transfer/population,
next-tick recruitment, actual Move/Flash/release, current-owner cell arrivals,
combat and pending-death population/slots, object callbacks, saved spatial intents,
explicit commands/flags, grouped and resumed replay, exact sufficient work bounds,
and consumed/invalid contexts. The combat fixture deliberately keeps the recruit
untagged and tests attached-object callbacks on a separate victim; it does not
bypass the tagged recruitment gate. These are authored test missions, not retail
playthroughs. See [provenance](../packages/sim/MISSION_WORLD_PROVENANCE.md).
