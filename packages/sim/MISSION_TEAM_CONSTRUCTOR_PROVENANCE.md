# Owned unit construction provenance

The new constructor catalog/history modules and original fixtures are
GPL-3.0-or-later WebRA2 code. They compose the genuine
[team activation](../content/TEAM_ACTIVATION_PROVENANCE.md),
[spawn catalog](TEAM_SPAWN_PROVENANCE.md),
[constructor prerequisites](MISSION_TEAM_CELL_PROVENANCE.md) and
[house ledger](MISSION_HOUSE_PROVENANCE.md). No code/table, dependency or retail
fixture is newly adopted.

This intermediate source/history checkpoint proves complete source unit membership,
initial count classification and structural birth provenance. Existing native proofs
remain bounded by their individual ledgers; it adds no native execution observation.
Atomic waypoint placement, permanent source-derived IDs and explicit birth/transfer
revision ordering are WebRA2 policies. Unit-only admission does not prove spawned
combat, infantry subcells, tag attachment or full mission behavior.

Distributions containing these modules must retain this notice, the composed
notices, [GPL text](../../LICENSES/GPL-3.0-or-later.txt), and corresponding source
and build instructions. The root MIT license does not relicense these modules.
